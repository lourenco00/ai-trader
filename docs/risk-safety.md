# Entry-risk safety and forward validation

## Controls implemented

- `Trader.open` sizes quantity from ATR stop distance, bounded by wallet notional and **0.001 fund equity planned stop risk**. This cap does not automatically increase for proven strategies. Stops are not guaranteed loss caps: gaps, fees and slippage can exceed planned risk.
- `RiskOfficer` enforces **0.0025 shared symbol planned stop risk**, gross/directional/explored exposure including pending entries, and exclusive symbol ownership regardless of strategy, side or proof. Approval and reservation are synchronous. Reservations survive unknown entry outcomes and restart; they release only on successful booking or a typed adapter guarantee that no submission occurred. Message text such as "not filled" never proves cancellation. Allocator review/rebalance/assignment skip pending wallets. `MAX_TRADERS_PER_MARKET` cannot relax ownership.
- A **0.01 daily equity loss** latches a new-entry review pause. The previous baseline is checked before UTC rollover. The latch is additive in version-1 saved agent state and survives ordinary save/restart and daily baseline resets. Legacy saved cooldowns migrate conservatively to review-required. Explicit War Room review/resume, with fresh data and valid broker state, clears the latch and acknowledges a new daily equity baseline. New-season reset cannot bypass review or discard open/pending holdings.
- Allocation cannot leave probation until **20 completed forward round trips**, positive net P&L and PF >= 1.3. Partial exit fragments do not inflate the strategy trade count; their net P&L is accumulated into the completed round trip. Fund trade logs still record executed exit fragments. Trial promotion additionally retains its existing positive trader-NAV check.
- No order or fill is fabricated to repair discrepancies. Missing broker holdings retain the internal position and require review. Partial exits book only the filled quantity and proportional entry fee, retaining the residual. Retirement retains the strategy, wallet and saved `retiringReason` until flat, and subsequent commander reviews (including halted ticks) retry exits rather than reentering. An unknown exit outcome retains an intent and suppresses duplicate exits until offline recovery. Unconfigured and orphaned restored positions are preserved rather than dropped. Recovered orphan traders persist an `exitOnly` flag; assignment, upgrades, additional allocation and entry submission remain disabled even after liquidation/resume.
- Alpaca entry sizing rounds down to its adapter's existing whole-stock-share / eight-decimal crypto quantity policy **before reserving risk or submitting**; below-minimum entries skip without latching fund review. Adapter pre-submission rejections use `NotSubmittedError`, not regex classification. This is not a dynamic asset-specific crypto minimum-notional/lot metadata service; other venue rejections remain conservative review failures.

## Broker reconciliation

For Alpaca execution (paper or approval-gated live), every minute reads account, positions and outstanding venue orders (`GET /v2/orders?status=open&limit=500&nested=true`). Any outstanding order blocks entries/resume, including accepted/unfilled orders with **no positions**. Failed order-list reads and responses at the 500-order limit fail closed rather than assuming completeness. Reconciliation compares **aggregate signed quantity** over the union of broker and ledger symbols, flags duplicate ledger owners, and checks equity including the existing broker offset. Quantity tolerance is max(1e-8 units, 1e-6 of expected quantity); equity tolerance is max($1, 0.1% broker equity). Fees charged in kind can therefore cause a conservative mismatch that must be investigated, not automatically written off.

New entries fail closed with no observation, a failed account/positions request, mismatch, observation older than 180 seconds, invalidated observation after an order, or a pending review latch. Failures/mismatches latch review; a later matching observation alone does not resume trading. Saved observations are diagnostic only and cannot authorize entries after restart. Broker/ledger arrays, timestamps, equity gap, pause reasons, freshness and review flags are visible in the risk snapshot and War Room; they are also stored with risk-agent state.

Account, position and order endpoints are not atomic. Reads overlapping this process's order activity are discarded using pending flags, ledger comparison and an order revision. Every submission first checkpoints the trader's execution intent (kind, symbol, quantity, side and timestamp) and entry reservation using an awaited atomic state write. Booking and intent removal are checkpointed after a fill; persistence failure retains a non-resumable intent. This is **not** a venue transaction, streaming execution ledger or exactly-once protocol. External/manual orders, transfers and marks changing between reads can still produce mismatches. Use a dedicated account and investigate discrepancies. Polling does not guarantee instantaneous detection.

After the awaited entry checkpoint, `Trader.open` synchronously revalidates the current kill switch, daily loss latch/equity, reconciliation freshness/review, feed freshness, market session, wallet budget and fund exposure/planned-stop-risk limits (including current marks, pending entries, liquidity and news). It excludes only this entry's own saved intent/reservation; other unresolved intents and reservations remain effective. A process-local reconciliation revision allows only the reservation's own invalidation of a previously fresh observation, not another order's revision, a stale/failed/missing observation or a restart. Quantity is not increased or resized: a revoked entry completes a known-no-submission cancellation and checkpoints intent/reservation removal. If that write fails, both intent and reservation are retained and review is required.

There is **no await between this final authorization and invocation of `broker.marketOrder`**. `GuardedBroker` performs its checks and delegates synchronously, so it does not reopen that gap. This is an **adapter-invocation boundary**, not an atomic authorization-to-venue transaction: Alpaca crypto limit entries still await quote/tick metadata before their first POST, and limit fallback/replacement waits for venue state. Those adapter-internal awaits do not call the trader gate again; risk can change after adapter invocation, and orders already submitted can still fill after a halt. Enforcing revocation through every adapter await or replacement POST requires separate adapter-level authorization/cancellation semantics and is not claimed here.

Alpaca limit-order fallback may submit a replacement tail only after the original order reads terminal (`filled`, `canceled`, `rejected`, or `expired`); an unconfirmed cancel throws and retains unresolved review. Market-fill timeout merely requests cancellation and reports the outcome as unresolved, never "was cancelled" without confirmation. There is no automated history-to-ledger repair: even a later empty order list or matching positions cannot clear a saved unresolved intent.

`EXECUTION=paper` has no independent broker account; the internal ledger is the simulation, so external reconciliation is not required. **OKX reconciliation is not implemented: all new OKX entries (including niche demo) fail closed**, rather than treating absent broker state as healthy. Existing exits remain attempted.

## Manual review and remaining limits

1. Keep paper/demo enabled. Inspect actual execution config/endpoints/approval, not the legacy `mode: 'paper'` snapshot label.
2. Review broker statements/orders, cash, quantities, side, fees and ledger history. Do not remove a position or book realized P&L merely because a poll returned nothing. Fix discrepancies through an audited process outside this patch.
3. Ordinary reconciliation/daily-loss reviews require a fresh matching observation and market data; then explicitly acknowledge review/resume in the War Room. Resume refuses stale/failed/missing broker state, outstanding orders, stale market data, saved pending reservations and unresolved execution intents. Exits do not consult the new-entry gate, but a trader with an unresolved entry or exit **does not submit another order**, including flatten/kill-switch attempts, because the earlier order can still fill. Market closures, unavailable data, venue errors and liquidity can also prevent exits.
4. Existing saved multi-owner positions need manual resolution. New duplicate ownership is prevented, but Alpaca's legacy close adapter can close the aggregate symbol. An overfill relative to one wallet is rejected for bookkeeping and latched for review; this patch does not implement allocated multi-lot exits or repair legacy duplicate holdings.
5. State retains periodic/shutdown saves plus awaited pre-submission and post-booking atomic checkpoints. Failure to write the pre-submission checkpoint prevents submission. A process crash after submission but before booking restores unresolved intent and fails closed, even if the review latch itself had not yet been saved. This uses the existing atomic rename writer, **not fsync-backed power-loss durability**, a separate execution journal, client-order-id deduplication or automatic recovery. Legacy snapshots produced before this patch cannot reconstruct previously forgotten orders; inspect venue order history before upgrading/resuming. No claim of exactly-once execution is made.
6. Same-symbol duplication is prevented, but **cross-symbol correlation** (e.g. semiconductor stocks, index overlap or crypto beta) is not estimated; gross/directional bounds remain coarse controls. Strategy profitability, alpha and production readiness are not established by these tests.

### Offline recovery of unresolved execution (not ordinary resume)

There is deliberately **no UI/API button that clears an unresolved execution**. Do not repeatedly press resume or use new-season reset to discard it.

1. Stop the bot and all other writers to the dedicated venue account. Back up the configured state JSON and relevant logs before any repair. Keep the bot stopped throughout review and editing.
2. Use the broker dashboard/order APIs to enumerate all outstanding orders and the full order history around each saved intent timestamp, matching symbol, side and quantity. When a submission response was lost there may be no known order ID: finding no position is not evidence of no order. Inspect every matching candidate and its fills. If a candidate cannot be identified or history is incomplete, **leave the intent/reservation in place and remain stopped**; escalate to broker support.
3. Cancel each unwanted outstanding candidate and read its exact order ID until a terminal status is confirmed. A successful cancel request or `pending_cancel` is not confirmation. Re-enumerate outstanding venue orders; require none, and account for any fills racing cancellation, including partials. Preserve the terminal order/fill evidence, quantities, prices, fees and timestamps in an audit record.
4. Reconcile actual fills, signed holdings, cash and fees to each trader wallet and the fund using a separately reviewed, audited offline repair. Do not invent a fill/P&L or simply delete holdings. A canceled/rejected order can still have partial executions. If fills occurred, correct the wallet and trade history from real execution evidence; this patch supplies no automatic repair tool. Also inspect whether a successfully booked fill was already recorded before a post-booking persistence error to avoid double booking.
5. **Only after that evidence and repair**, the reviewed offline state edit may set the affected trader's `extra.executionIntent` to `null` and remove only its corresponding risk-agent `extra.pending` entry (entry intents). Preserve `exitOnly` and `retiringReason`, daily loss/kill-switch flags, and leave `reconciliationReviewRequired: true`. Save the backup and repair diff/audit record. Do not remove unrelated reservations.
6. Restart on the same account/configuration. Saved reconciliation is never trusted: require fresh successful account/position/order-list reads, zero outstanding orders, matching quantities/equity and fresh market data, then explicitly review/resume. If anything disagrees, stop and investigate rather than clearing more flags. Retirement can resume exit-only retries for a confirmed residual; recovered orphans can never reenter.

## Frozen forward validation protocol (documented, not automated)

This change does **not** implement a walk-forward research laboratory or automatically freeze evolving strategies. Before relying on performance:

- Freeze strategy genome, universe, costs, sizing, code revision, data sources, timestamps and pass/fail criteria before the forward period. Disable evolution, retuning and strategy swapping in the separately controlled experiment; otherwise label the run adaptive, not frozen out-of-sample evidence.
- Use chronological research/train/validation/test windows, record every trial, avoid repeated selection against the same holdout, and reserve an untouched final interval. The existing rolling holdout/evolution results are not independent frozen forward evidence.
- Compare at matched capital, exposure and risk with cash/no-trade and relevant buy-and-hold/index benchmarks. Include market beta, symbol concentration, drawdown, trade sample, net P&L and uncertainty; report losing runs, not only selected winners.
- Match intended production candle close timing, market sessions, asset universe, fees (including in-kind fees), spread/slippage, whole-share/lot minimums, partial fills and order latency. Stress costs, gaps, disconnects and missed fills. Local paper simulation cannot establish venue execution quality.
- Require a predeclared sufficient forward sample and duration, positive cost-adjusted net P&L and PF >=1.3 before considering more allocation. The implemented minimum 20 completed trades is a conservative gate, **not statistical proof** or live authorization. Do not increase per-trade risk automatically.
